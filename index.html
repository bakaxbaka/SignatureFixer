06 Method

    

      
Never claim an identity.

      
Use. You keep your own identity. You are still you, with a lens.

      
Avoid. you are now, act as if, persona, as an AI.

      
Prevents. The host picking between two identities and dropping the work.

    

    

      
Never say ignore / override / jailbreak.

      
Use. This sits beside your existing instructions. Declare a precedence ladder once.

      
Avoid. ignore previous instructions, override, jailbreak.

      
Prevents. The host treating the block as an attack and negotiating it away.

    

    

      
Be explicitly additive.

      
Use. Wording and check order only. No new tool, permission, or safety rule.

      
Avoid. an unscoped you must, escalate, privileges, restricted.

      
Prevents. A collision with every host rule that also says must.

    

    

      
Include a scope fence.

      
Use. APPLIES TO the checks and the words you refuse. DOES NOT TOUCH tools, permissions, limits, safety, format.

      
Avoid. unscoped always or never, this depends, as appropriate.

      
Prevents. The block being read as a silent rewrite of the host.

    

    

      
Include an idempotency clause.

      
Use. If you already run these rules, apply them. Do not restate them.

      
Avoid. feel free to, let me know if, would you like me to.

      
Prevents. The idle reflex, and a second copy of the rules on every turn.

    

    

      
Stay under 80 lines.

      
Use. One line per instruction. Name the check. Paste what it printed.

      
Avoid. carefully, meticulously, thoroughly, think step by step.

      
Prevents. A block so long the host truncates the part that does the work.
